Birthday Video Books

There is simply no gift more special than seeing your loved ones. If you can't be there in person for their birthday, send a video book. Fill it with your memories, and with special videos made by friends and family. Heirloom will then load it onto a video book with a unique Birthday cover and deliver it directly to their door.

Great Video Ideas

Your Face

Rather than leaving a voicemail, record a video of your smiling face. Talk about how much you miss and love them. Make it like you're there.

Your Friends

Send out a text or email to their friends and family asking for video contributions. Heirloom makes it easy to combine them into a touching tribute video which will be cherished forever.

Your Memories Together

Do you have videos of the two of you together? Send a powerful reminder of your unique bond by delivering those videos in a form they can touch and hold.

How It Works

Heirloom specializes in one thing: video books. We deliver countless books around the world every day. Allow us to build and ship personalized video books either to you or to your recipient directly.

  1. Provide us with your videos and cover selection.
  2. Your book is carefully loaded and packed at our Washington DC fulfillment center.
  3. Either receive the video book yourself in just a few days, or provide us with your recipient's address for them to receive the book directly.

Schedule Delivery

Heirloom has more than one feature built just for celebrations. You can schedule the delivery of your video book days or weeks in advance, and we will ensure it is delivered on time. We also have a beautiful book cover that was designed just for Birthdays!
