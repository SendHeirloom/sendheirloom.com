Why should I offer video books?

There are two great ways to use video books in your business:

  1. Bundle Video Books

    By including video books with your videography packages, you can ensure your customers will get to enjoy (and share) their wedding video. The tangible reminder of your services helps to ensure invoices are paid on time, and that the referrals come rolling in!

  2. Sell Video Books

    For every wedding guest who was able to attend, there is one who could not. Allow your customers to connect with their missed loved ones by offering video books shipped directly to their loved ones. With a simple list of names and addresses Heirloom can ship any number of books directly to their recipients.
