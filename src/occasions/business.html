Video Books for Business

A business video book is a physical book with an integrated video player that automatically plays your videos and photos when the cover is opened. They come in various sizes--from 10 mins to 4 hrs-- and have been described as the perfect marketing tool.


No wifi is needed. Simply open the book to play the crisp, high-quality videos, photos, and music. Pick from 16 cover designs, or design your custom-printed front, back, spine and inside cover.
