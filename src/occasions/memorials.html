Memorial Video Books

{% image "./src/static/images/hands-blue.jpg", "Holding card", "325, 300", "(min-width: 450px) 630, 375" %}

We created Heirloom to allow our customers to use the power of video to connect with their loved ones. To our surprise, however, many of those customers had an even more pressing need: to commemorate and remember a loved one who has been lost. We are now honored to offer memorial video books.

How It Works

  1. Provide us with your videos and cover selection.
  2. Your book is lovingly loaded and packed at our Bethesda, MD fulfillment center.
  3. Either receive the video books yourself in just a few days, or provide us with your recipient's address for them to receive the books directly.
