Memorial Video Books

We created Heirloom to allow our customers to use the power of video to connect with their loved ones. To our great surprise, however, many of those customers had an even more pressing need: to commemorate and remember a loved one who has been lost.

We are now honored to offer memorial video books. Each one is delivered with respect and faith in the power of a video to reconnect us with our loved one's memory.

Memorials Are Different

We treat memorial orders differently from the other video book orders we receive. Memorial customers receive more attention, more care, and more support.

The X family had a deep need to commemorate their lost son. Unfortunately what they didn't have is a copy of the video memorial which was aired for him on a local news station. We were able to reach out to the station's production team to find the video.

Their story brought us to tears, and represent a perfect vision for why we provide memorial customers with the attention they deserve.

How It Works

Heirloom specializes in one thing: video books. We deliver countless books around the world every day. Allow us to build and ship personalized video books either to you, or to your friends and family directly.

  1. Provide us with your videos and cover selection.
  2. Your book is lovingly loaded and packed at our Washington DC fulfillment center.
  3. Either receive the video books yourself in just a few days, or provide us with your recipient's address for them to receive the books directly.

Why Video

We sit in a privileged position. We are entrusted with countless videos of loved ones. Through that experience it has become clear that seeing someone talk, laugh, think, and play, is more than just an alternative to photos. It has the unique ability to connect us with those we've lost.
