What’s a video book?

A physical book mailed to loved ones filled with meaningful videos of your family and friends.

The videos play automatically when the cover is opened, making them perfect gifts for people without smartphones.

About the books.

Video books come fully charged and loaded with your home videos. Your recipient just opens it and your videos play.

  • If you can open a greeting card, you can receive a video book.
  • No technical knowledge, computer, smartphone, or Internet is required.
  • 5” HD screen, delivered directly to your recipient already loaded with up to 10 min of your home movies.
