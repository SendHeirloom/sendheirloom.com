Frequently asked questions

What is an Heirloom video book?

An Heirloom video book is a physical book with an integrated video player which gets sent in the mail. You select videos in our app and we do all the hard work of shipping a video-playing book right to your loved-one.

How do I upload my video?

To begin, simply download the Heirloom app from the Apple App Store. Open the Heirloom app and select the videos you want to share from your video library. The Heirloom app makes it easy to drag-and-drop the videos you want and lets you preview the video before you finish customizing your Heirloom video book.

Are there limits to the size of the videos I can send?

The Heirloom video book will accommodate up to about 10 minutes of HD video.

How do I place my order?

At the moment Heirloom video books can only be ordered from our iPhone app. First install the Heirloom app onto your iPhone and open it. Once you’ve selected your videos and customized your book, you enter the name of the recipient, then checkout and you’re done. Heirloom supports both credit cards and Apple Pay.

How much does an Heirloom video book cost?

We charge $49 including shipping within the US. Video players are expensive, but we have worked hard to make Heirloom as affordable as possible.

Can I order an Heirloom video book from my computer or iPad? Do you support Android devices?

We are a family startup working hard to make Heirloom a reality. Unfortunately we have not yet had the time or resources to support ordering from the web, iPads, or Android devices, but we do hope to soon. Please feel free to email us and we are happy to update you as soon as we can support your preferred device.

How long will it take to process my order?

Heirloom typically processes and ships orders within 24-48 hours. We generally use USPS shipping for your finished video book. Due to the current COVID pandemic, shipping dates are at-best approximate and can't be guaranteed, unfortunately.

Do you offer rush shipping?

We’ll do our best to rush your order. If you need your order rushed, please email us at help@sendheirloom.com.

Do you ship internationally?

Not yet, but we want to! Please send us an email and we're happy to update you when your country is supported.

Are your shipments safe from COVID-19?

The CDC currently advises that catching COVID from a package is unlikely. Nothing is perfectly safe from contamination, however. We suggest that everyone allow their packages to sit for several days before opening them, and that they wash their hands promptly after touching the packaging and its contents.

If I have a video file on a thumb drive or my computer, can I mail it to you?

We are only able to accept videos via the Heirloom app at this point.

How does the recipient charge the Heirloom video book?

Your recipient will not need to charge anything to watch your video. When the book is opened the video immediately and automatically plays. The book should play for at least a full hour (over six repetitions of the longest video length we support) without charging. Should it become necessary to watch it further, simply plug the enclosed cable USB into an outlet adapter or right into a computer to recharge the battery, and return the book to them.

Does my recipient need to have a computer or phone? Do they need Internet or WiFi?

No! Heirloom video books start playing your video(s) the moment they are opened. The books do not need to be initially charged or interfaced with technology in any way. If you can look at a photo, you can watch an Heirloom video book.

Do Heirloom video books support playing my video’s sound?

Yes, our Heirloom video books include a tiny speaker which will play any sound recorded with your video.

What are the technical specs of the video player?

The player uses a 5" HD IPS LCD screen with a resolution of 854x480. It includes 256 MB of memory, which translates to over ten minutes of video in most cases. The book also integrates a USB-rechargable battery which lasts for approximately one hour of playback, and a single speaker.

What’s your return policy?

Our books are a custom product made just for your recipient which limits our ability to provide a refund on delivered orders. If you are not satisfied for any reason however, please contact us and we will make it right. We will also happily replace any book that has technical issues, however. Please contact us at help@sendheirloom.com to receive return authorization.

How do I track my order?

Your tracking number will be included in the email you receive when your order is confirmed. Feel free to send us an email with any tracking related questions to help@sendheirloom.com.

Can I recycle my Heirloom video book?

Absolutely. Please mail the book back to us at 4200 Wisconsin Ave. NW #106-274, Washington DC 20016 and we will happily recycle it.

Can I load new video onto my Heirloom video book?

Yes, we want you to get as much value from your Heirloom video book as you possibly can. If you are interested in manually loading your book feel free to connect it to any computer and transfer your video files just as you would load them onto a USB thumbdrive. Please be aware that we cannot accept responsibility or offer support for any book or video which has been loaded in this way.

Where can I obtain a PNG/SVG of the Heirloom logo, product photography, or other media?

Visit our press downloads page.

How do I contact Heirloom with questions or suggestions?

Heirloom is a family-ran startup that welcomes your questions and is eager to hear any ideas to improve our service and product. Please email us at help@sendheirloom.com with any questions and feedback and we will respond shortly.
