  **What is a Wedding Video Book?**  

A video book is an unusual keepsake that reproduces the spirit of an old photograph album, in a real multimedia experience. Compared to digital file formats placed on your phone or cloud storage, a video book represents a tangible object that one can hold, share and safely store. Open like a book and  watch the magic begin. The embedded screen automatically plays your wedding videos and photos, including the audio tracks and background music.
