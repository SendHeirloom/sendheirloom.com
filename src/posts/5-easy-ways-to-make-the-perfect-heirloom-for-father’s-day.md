Heirloom is here to help make that special bond with your dad stronger. We want to make it easy for you to share how you feel about your dad on Father’s Day. What better way to share how you feel than with a keepsake Dad can keep forever.

#### Here are 5 ideas to build the perfect Father’s Day video book

1. ###### Record a message from you.

Stuck on what to say? Try these prompts:

* What’s something you love that Dad always does?
* What’s a memory you have with Dad that makes you laugh?
* What’s an activity you always look forward to with your dad?
* Wish him a Happy Father’s Day!

2. #### Record Happy Father’s Day messages from your friends and family.

Stuck on how to do this? Try this:

* Message your friends and family to record a Happy Father’s Day message to your Dad. (Make sure it’s recorded with the screen tilted horizontally!)
* Have them email you the video greeting.
* Upload the video clips to the [website video book builder](create.sendheirloom.com). 
* Ship Dad the video book!



3. #### Upload clips from all your most recent moments you have together.

* Find all your favorite recent clips with Dad (from the last 1-5 years)
* Upload the video clips to the [website video book builder](create.sendheirloom.com). Up to 10 min of video.
* Mail Dad’s video book!



4. #### Still have VHS cassettes? Upload memorable clips from your childhood together.

* Get the cassettes digitized at a local Walgreens, CVS, or Photo Center or use a digitizing service.
* Upload the video clips to the [website video book builder](create.sendheirloom.com). Up to 10 min of video.  



5. #### A fun combination of these ideas!

Here’s a quick sample script for a Father’s Day video book:

* “Hey Dad! Happy Father’s Day. I made this video book for you to remind you of how much I appreciate you. Here are a few of my favorite moments with you…”
* \*Videos from childhood together\*
* \*Videos from the last 3 years together\*
* “I cherish these memories, and I know you do too.”
* “I’m not the only one who appreciates you!”
* \*Happy Father’s Day Greetings from friends and family\*
* “Happy Father’s Day Dad! Cheers for being the best Dad I could ever ask for!”

Make this Father’s Day stand out as special by sharing some meaningful moments and messages.**
