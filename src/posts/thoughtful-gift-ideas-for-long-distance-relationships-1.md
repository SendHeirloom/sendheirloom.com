It seems that being in a relationship that is challenged by many miles of separation now only has a name but an acronym: long distance relationship (LDR). The key to success, whether for a limited time or way longer, may be the individuals' ability to see the unique opportunities, including how to express love. Here we consider actions, words, deeds and gifts that test your creativity. Whether for a specific celebration such as the couple's anniversary, a birthday apart, or a "just because" reason, there are unique gifts and unique ways to build a bridge between the couple until you are once again together.

Presented are a collection of gift ideas for a couple seeking to strengthen their bond, organized to fit everyone's targets. Personalized items solidify in writing, literally, the couple's bond. Experiences are suggested, showing that just because you are separated by miles you can still celebrate together. Gifts for techy folks are offered for fun and to show that tech tools can offer some bonding opportunities. Fun gifts are suggested to keep the feelings light and celebrate each other's sense of humor. And, for those with come crafty interest, several of the gifts offer ranges of guidance from the most artistic to those willing to follow directions all the way through their project.

**Personalized Items**

Personalized gifts are basically those you "can't return" because they have specific names, dates and even pictures that are unique to the recipient. These items are set to be enjoyed way beyond the special event.

Consider the impact you will have when you compile special photos and videos into a personalized book that. Yes, you will recreating your special moments from the first date to that special adventure. And, if you are fortunate to have already married, you can invite your spouse to relive that amazing day with the creation of a [Heirloom Wedding Video Book](https://sendheirloom.com). This keepsake will be cherished as you preserve your feelings and share it with others for a lifetime and beyond.

**Custom Map Art**

**Personalized Jewelry**

![Long Distance Necklace](https://ucarecdn.com/340d5df1-66db-4911-a7ea-23941c0028a4/-/resize/1560x/-/stretch/off/-/format/auto/-/progressive/yes/ "LongDistance")

Take the tradition of [engraved jewelry](https://www.madebymary.com/collections/personalized-jewelry) to a new level by uniting your initials, your special date, or even a very meaningful phrase. While small is size your message will hold the grandeur it represents and keep you close. Knowing your significant other best, you will choose the right piece from necklaces and rings to bracelets, earrings and more.

**Experiences**

There is no escaping the wish that long distance couples wish they could be together more often, almost always. Often its for the routine parts of our lives, and often for those special adventures. Shared experiences, even if shared while still far apart, can do wonders to strengthen bonds. Here are some ideas to create memorable moments together:

**Virtual Cooking Classes**

![Virtual Cooking Class](https://ucarecdn.com/48029e2c-51b2-4b12-8d0e-f17b57735e08/-/resize/1560x/-/stretch/off/-/format/auto/-/progressive/yes/ "Long Distance Gifts")

Consider how you both love to eat, and perhaps even cook. Find a [virtual cooking class](https://www.cozymeal.com/online-cooking-classes) that will place you both in the "kitchen" prepare the same meal together over video call. The pre-planning including shopping and prep of equipment can begin the fun and then imagine all the laughs as you share the experience and then the prize: enjoying a meal "together."

**Online Game Night**

The wonders of our techy world can come alive when you choose from the vast variety of [online games](https://www.cozymeal.com/online-cooking-classes) ready for your and your significant other to play together. Perhaps you will begin with am old favorite, then move to solving puzzles or competing in trivia. When getting and staying connected is the goal the choses are easy. Both of you will surely be winners with this experience.

**Streaming Movie Night**

Those streaming service platforms just keep getting better and easier. Consider [Teleparty](https://www.teleparty.com/) (formerly Netflix Party) for the newest way to enjoy television shows and movies with your long distance friends, in real-time. You can enhance the fun by pre-planning the popcorn treats, cozy PJs and favorite beverages.

**Conclusion**

Long-distance relationships come with their own set of challenges, but thoughtful gifts can help keep the connection strong. From personalized mementos and shared experiences to tech gadgets and funny surprises, there's something for every couple.
