**Prepare to Celebrate Father’s Day**

It’s not surprising that dads treasure the Father’s Day cards they receive. They keep
them for a long time; longer than other gifts, and much longer than you may have
expected. We all might suspect that he is reliving and enjoying the sentiments they
express. It’s time to starting planning how you will show your appreciation and love to
Dad this year.


**A Delicious Celebration**
Perhaps a good place to start is how will you treat him to a special Father’s Day meal.
Breakfast or brunch time is always a good start. Maybe you can create a homemade
breakfast spread or organize a picnic meal. Of course, many restaurants offer special
celebrations on this day. Just make sure to plan ahead by securing the reservation, the
menu ingredients or the perfect spot in nature.


**An Active Celebration**
Now you can proceed toward the activities that Dad enjoys the most: a stroll, a more
challenging hike, a round of golf, an artist pursuit or watching a movie together. If Dad is
a music lover, look around for a local live music concert at a venue in your community.


**A Playful Celebration**
No matter our age, playing games with our Dad is a timeless activity. How about
organizing a game day or night? You can keep it simple by pulling out the classic board
games or sticking with a deck of cards. Or, you can begin slow and add more
challenging competitions, including some that take advantage of technology. And, to
increase the fun and excitement, expand your guest list to include many friends and
family members, inviting all generations.
