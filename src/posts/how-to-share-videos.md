Thanks to the invention of smartphones, that literally fit in the palm of our hand, most of us have almost instantly become avid video producers! We film ourselves, and especially our little children, nearly every day. The sharing of these precious films can be as quick and easy as it was to create them, when using the right tool for yourself and your future audience. Here are some of the easiest and fastest ways to learn How to Share Videos.

***Share Digitally***

Learning how to share videos digitally, be they long or short, professionally filmed or family-credited, is quicker and easier when you learn about various options and apply the one best suited for your tech skills and recipient's equipment. Presented are several methods, many of which are nearly the same as sending text messages or emailing photos. Consider the size of your production, usually length and enhanced quality, as well as your recipients’ tech knowledge and devices as you review these current options.

**Send Your Video in a Text Message**

Once your production is created, the video can be shared in a text message. From your phone or other device, select the video to share and enter your recipients’ smartphone number. Within seconds they will receive this new message, complete with your name and ready for viewing.

**Deliver Your Video by Email**

Much like text messaging, you can easily learn how to share videos to a recipient’s email address. The simple steps begin with the selection of your videos and click on the “send” icon. Follow the prompt to enter the recipient’s email address. Adding a funny subject line, even a long message, can add to their excitement. Just wait for their very appreciative reply.

**Use a Video Sharing Service**

The use of one of several well-developed video sharing services can easily provide the tools required to learn how to share videos. The most popular services include:

**Google Drive**

Storing and organizing your videos on [Google Drive](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2423694?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) offers easy access and speedy sharing. Create a direct email to recipients, including the selected videos and messages. Additionally, take advantage of the opportunity to set permissions for each recipient, ranging from view only, to comment or even to become an editor. Or, you may choose to generate a sharable link and utilized the imbedded tools to edit the show.

**Dropbox**

Storing your videos on [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/official-teams-page?_tk=paid_sem_goog_biz_pmax_int20&_camp=20803912268&_kw=|&_ad=||c&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=20803912736&gbraid=0AAAAADubkg_oTmpU-uobekqkbItr2aqUi&gclid=Cj0KCQjwgvnCBhCqARIsADBLZoKjwSWl_nO4nNTQIEjPd2nVTVMtzJotSIHgwsB0JFu_RaqC7mrkJ_UaAvS1EALw_wcB) offers several specific advantages. The tool allows for the storage and sharing of video films from short to long and very long files, as well as uncompressed video files. Your recipients will enjoy the ease of viewing your productions, also providing the option for their downloading of the videos, even without establishing their own account.

**Share Your Video on YouTube**

Do you enjoy watching YouTube long movies? Did you know that millions of short family clips are also stored there? You can become a [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/creators/how-things-work/getting-started/) video poster too! Start by signing into YouTube. Near the top you will find the “create post” button, and that’s your first step. Enter a message, add your video and click “post”. Your video is ready for viewing. Of course, only if your audience knows that it is posted. So remember to let your audience know by sharing your new YouTube video link for their quick and often enjoyment.

***Share Physically***

Beyond the virtual sharing methods described above, there are several clever options that offer expanded benefits for your recipients. Consider these tools and the simplified instructions provided to learn to share videos.

**Mail Your Videos in a Video-Playing Book**

Perhaps you want to preserve these precious videos and are frustrated and concerned that your devices, your storage tools, and even your hardware, will fail and all is lost. And, think for a moment when you last searched for a photo or videos known to be stored, only to have the exciting time to see and share it pass by. You simply could not locate the media. For a practical solution to this dilemma for yourself, your family and friends, check out [Heirloom Video Books](http://www.sendheirloom.com/). This clever product receives your videos through their website, creates a personalized book, and sends it quickly to your very lucky recipient. Grandparents, parents, children and everyone in between will lift the cover and be instantly surprised to see the video playing, right in their hands. That’s right, no electronics to use, no electronics to know how to use, and no password to forget. 

**Load to a Thumb Drive**

The device known as a thumb drive or USB flash drive is plugged into a USB port on your computer. These ports are most often located on the sides of a laptop and on the front or back of a desktop computer. This signals your computer to accept your commands to copy specific files that have been loaded to the computer to the now plugged in external drive.

So here you have it! Seven ways to easily share your videos. No more excuses, only the challenge of selecting which to share today!
