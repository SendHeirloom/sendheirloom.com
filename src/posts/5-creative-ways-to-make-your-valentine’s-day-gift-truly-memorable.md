## 1. Capture Your Love Story

A **custom [Heirloom video book](https://sendheirloom.com)** lets you compile photos and videos from your relationship, from your first date to your most recent adventures together. This turns ordinary memories into a **sentimental keepsake** that your partner will treasure forever.

## 2. Include Heartfelt Messages

Adding a personal note or recording a video message is a simple way to make your gift **meaningful and unique**. Whether it’s a romantic “I love you” or a funny inside joke, these messages make your **video gift** truly one-of-a-kind.

## 3. Celebrate Milestones

Mark special occasions like anniversaries, trips, or even your favorite shared hobbies. Highlighting these moments in a **video memory book** shows thoughtfulness and attention to the details that make your relationship special.

## 4. Get Friends and Family Involved

For a truly unique gift, ask friends or family members to contribute short video clips or messages. This is perfect for long-distance relationships or for couples who love celebrating with a community of loved ones.

## 5. Make It a Surprise

Surprise your partner by presenting the **video book** on Valentine’s Day or mailing it directly to them. Even a last-minute gift can be meaningful when it’s filled with curated memories and personal touches.
