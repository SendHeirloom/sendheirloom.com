# Thoughtful Gift Ideas for Long Distance Relationships

It seems that being in a relationship that is challenged by many miles of separation now only has a name but an acronym: long-distance relationship (LDR).



The key to success, whether for a limited time or way longer, may be the individuals' ability to see the unique opportunities, including how to express love. Here we consider actions, words, deeds and gifts that test your creativity. Whether for a specific celebration such as the couple's anniversary, a birthday apart, or a "just because" reason, there are unique gifts and unique ways to build a bridge between the couple and until you are once again together.

Here are a collection of gift ideas for a couple seeking to strengthing their bond, organized to fit everyone's targets. Personalized items are presented to solidify in writering, literally, the couple's bond. Experiences are suggested, showing that just because you are separated by miles you can still celebrate together. Gifts for techy folks are offered for fun and to show that tech tools can offer some bonding opportunities. Fun gifts are suggested to keep the feelings light and celebrate each other's sense of humor. And, for those with come crafty interesting, several of the gifts offer ranges of guidance from the most artistic to those willing to follow directions all the way through their project.

## Experiences

There is no escaping the wish that long distance couples wish they could be together more often, almost always. Often its for the routine parts of our lives, and often for those special adventures. Shared experiences, even if shared while still far apart, can do wonders to strengthen bonds. Here are some ideas to create memorable moments together:



* **Virtual Cooking Classes**: Consider how you both love to eat, and perhaps even cook. Find a [virtual cooking class](https://www.cozymeal.com/online-cooking-classes) that will place you both in the "kitchen" prepare the same meal together over video call. The pre-planning including shopping and prep of equipment can begin the fun and then imagine all the laughs as you share the experience and then the prize: enjoying a meal "together."
* **Online Game Night**: The wonders of our techy world can come alive when you choose from the vast variety of [online games](https://www.cozymeal.com/online-cooking-classes) ready for your and your significant other to play together. Perhaps you will begin with am old favorite, then move to solving puzzles or competing in trivia. When getting and staying connected is the goal the choses are easy. Both of you will surely be winners with this experience.
