How recently have you viewed your wedding video? The answer is probably the same as almost every couple: "It has been a while, a long while".\
\
Or, perhaps you are engaged? Here is the truth: You and your spouse won't be watching your wedding video nearly as often as you would expect. Warning: spoilers ahead.\
\
Most couples will tell you that one of the most significant mementos from their wedding day is their wedding video. They say it's really the only way to experience those unforgettable moments again.\
\
The reality is that it actually requires more work than it is worth to wait for your files to load and recover. Pictures are simple. To begin reliving the moments, simply place the wedding album on your coffee table.

It's time to treat your wedding video in the same way!\
\
At Heirloom we have developed a straightforward, inventive, and remarkable method for watching your wedding film again and again.\
\
Have your movie available whenever you want with our [wedding video book](https://sendheirloom.com/wedding-video-book/). Showoff the enchantment of your big day to yourself or your visitors by placing it on your coffee table next to your wedding album.\
\
Our design offers a big surprise: Simply open the cover of this video wedding book, it will look and feel just like any other traditional album. The surprise is revealed when you lift the cover of your Heirloom wedding book and your video plays instantly and smoothly, thanks to embedded audio.  \
\
You can view your wedding movie as often and whenever you would like on your Heirloom Video Book. No internet connection is required.
