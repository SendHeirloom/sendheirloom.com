## **The Book That Changed How We Share Memories**

![](https://ucarecdn.com/0af3c6c0-6725-4d7e-8a8e-a5bf020b9d6c/-/resize/1560x/-/stretch/off/-/format/auto/-/progressive/yes/)

What started as a simple gift for our 92-year-old grandmother turned into a product featured on the **TODAY Show, Good Morning America, CBS News, QVC**, and in the hands of tens of thousands of families across the country.

So what *is* a video book—and why is it showing up on nightstands, coffee tables, and national TV?

- - -

### **What’s a Video Book?**

A Heirloom Video Book looks like a hardcover photo album. But when you open it, it automatically plays your chosen videos and photos—no apps, no Wi-Fi, no tech hurdles. Just instant playback of the moments that matter most.

It’s more than a book. It’s a time capsule.

* Choose from **5” or 7” HD screens**
* Load up to **1 hour of media**
* Add a **custom cover and message**
* Use it **again and again**

Whether you're celebrating a wedding, remembering a loved one, or saying “I miss you” from miles away—Heirloom gives you a tangible way to keep those moments close.

- - -

### **Why We Created Heirloom**

At the height of the pandemic, my brother Zack and I realized our grandma couldn’t stream videos or browse social media. So we built her something she *could* use: a simple book that played video when opened.

Her joy—and her tears—showed us we had something meaningful.

Today, our mission is the same: to help people stay connected through thoughtful, physical storytelling.

We believe:

* **Technology should feel human**
* **Gifts should feel personal**
* **Memories should be seen and heard—not just stored**

- - -

### **From Family Keepsake to National Recognition**

Since that first video book, we’ve made some big moves:

* Launched our website and custom builder
* Appeared on **The TODAY Show**, **GMA**, **QVC**, and **CBS News**
* Partnered with Walmart, Kohl’s, The Paper Store, and Shutterfly
* Sold over **$1 million** in video books
* Introduced **vertical video formats**, **custom covers**, and **DIY kits**
* Expanded into **corporate gifts and B2B video books**

We even developed our own software and internal fulfillment system to scale fast and stay personal. Every book is still built with care—just like the first one.
