**Hold Santa’s Personal Message in Your Hands!**\
The magic of the holiday season just got even more special!

![](https://ucarecdn.com/c84ba326-4d2e-40cc-9e21-316d7156bdf9/-/resize/1560x/-/stretch/off/-/format/auto/-/progressive/yes/)

We’re thrilled to announce a heartwarming partnership between Portable North Pole and Heirloom Video Books. Now, you can pair your custom Santa video—complete with your loved one’s name—with a video-playing book to treasure the moment forever.

Portable North Pole and Heirloom Video Books have come together to help you save, store, and share your personalized Santa video in a way that tells your story and captures the joy of the season.

 **What is Portable North Pole?**

[Portable North Pole](https://www.portablenorthpole.com/?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=heirloom&) creates super realistic and personalized video messages from Santa. They’re so magical, even the adults might start believing in Santa again!

[Heirloom Video Books](http://sendheirloom.com/) are custom-made, video-playing books that bring your memories to life. When you open the cover, your favorite moments—from family trips to everyday snapshots—are played on a 5” HD screen with sound. With 16 beautiful cover options (including 4 holiday designs), or the option to create your own custom cover, these books are both a keepsake and a gift!

No special tech, Wi-Fi, or internet needed—just open the book and enjoy high-quality videos of your cherished memories. Plus, it’s reloadable and comes with a rechargeable battery, making it a perfect way to celebrate Christmas or any special occasion!

 **The Perfect Blend: Messages and Memories**

Now, picture this: your loved one receives a video message from Santa himself, calling them by name! Add your favorite photos and videos to the mix—moments that made you smile, laugh, or cry—and you’ve got a priceless memory captured in a physical video book.

This is the first time Portable North Pole and Heirloom Video Books have joined their strengths to bring the holiday magic to life in such a personal and meaningful way. Your unique Santa message, along with your cherished moments, will surely become a treasured keepsake that you can relive over and over again.

**Why This Partnership is So Special**

The magic of this partnership lies in how beautifully Portable North Pole and Heirloom Video Books complement each other. Portable North Pole brings the voice of the holidays, while Heirloom Video Books captures your visual memories in a way that’s easy to enjoy time and time again. Together, they create something extraordinary—a living memory you can hold, hear, and see.

Ready to create something unforgettable? Head over to Portable North Pole and Heirloom Video Books today to build your custom message and video book. This holiday season, give the gift of memories that will last a lifetime.
