Returns

Returns & Exchanges

  • You may request a return or exchange within 30 days of delivery if your Video Book is defective, damaged, or not as described.
  • Items must be returned in their original packaging, with all included accessories.

How to Return or Exchange:

  • Contact us at help@sendheirloom.com with your order number, description of the issue, and photos if applicable
  • We will review and either authorize the return or offer a replacement.
  • If approved, we’ll send you instructions and a return shipping label if the issue is our error

Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds will be issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days
  • If only part of your order is returned, refund will cover only the returned portion.

Warranty Coverage

  • All Video Books come with a 1-year limited warranty covering defects in materials or workmanship
  • This warranty does not cover damage from misuse, alterations, accidents, water damage, or unauthorized repair.

If your product has a defect covered under warranty:

  • Contact us at help@sendheirloom.com with your order number, description of the defect, and photos or video evidence.
  • We will repair or replace the product at no cost to you.

Non-Returnable & Exempt Items

  • Personalized/custom items (unless they arrive defective) cannot be returned for change of mind.
  • Gift cards are non-refundable.
  • Final sale or clearance items (marked as such) cannot be returned or exchanged.

Additional Rights

  • These policies are in addition to any rights you may have under consumer protection laws in your country or region.
  • If you are a California resident or under regional law with additional consumer rights, our policies comply where applicable.
